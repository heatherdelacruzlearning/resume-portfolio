# About Heather DelaCruz

Senior Learning Experience Designer, eLearning Developer, and LMS Administrator with 15+ years of experience designing and delivering digital learning solutions for workforce development, client education, and technology adoption.

My work sits at the intersection of instructional design, digital content development, enterprise learning technology, and practical implementation. I help organizations translate complex processes and technical information into learning experiences that are understandable, engaging, and actionable.

## My approach

I focus on learning that makes real work easier:

- building learner-centered solutions for product adoption and skill development
- turning technical and operational knowledge into structured learning experiences
- creating meaningful, accessible content across multiple learning formats
- supporting the full learning lifecycle, from design through LMS administration and reporting

## Career perspective

Across healthcare technology, member education, and workforce learning, I have designed and supported training initiatives that build competency and confidence among diverse learner groups. My work combines creative instructional design with practical operational understanding so that learning experiences are not only polished, but also effective in the real world.

## Strengths

- Learning experience design
- eLearning development
- LMS administration and configuration
- Storyboarding and assessment design
- Multimedia training and instructional video
- Cross-functional stakeholder collaboration
- Process improvement and operational support
