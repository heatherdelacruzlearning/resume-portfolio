# Tools & Technologies

I work across the intersection of instructional design, digital learning, LMS administration, and learning operations. The tools below reflect the platforms and technologies I use to develop, deliver, and support effective learning experiences.

---

## eLearning Authoring & Content Development

### Articulate Rise 360
Used for responsive, scalable digital learning experiences and structured content development. Ideal for onboarding, microlearning, client education, and certification-based learning.

### Articulate Storyline 360
Used for interactive, scenario-based learning, branching activities, assessments, and more complex learning experiences that require logic-based design.

### Adobe Captivate
Used for software demonstrations, interactive modules, screen-based learning, and multimedia course development.

### Adobe Premiere Pro
Used for instructional video production, screen recordings, audio editing, captions, and supporting visual assets.

## LMS Administration & Learning Platforms

### Skilljar / Gainsight
Used for client education, learning paths, certification programs, and learner management in enterprise learning environments.

Used for enterprise learning administration, content publishing, tracking, and learning operations support.

### myLearningPointe
Used for learner access, account management, reporting, subscription workflows, and continuing education support.

Used for platform configuration, course management, learner support, and long-term LMS administration.

### Totara
Used for learning administration, course management, and learning operations support.

### SCORM / xAPI
Used to ensure content compatibility and tracking across LMS environments.
