# Case Study: Healthcare Technology Product Training

**Netsmart Technologies | 2019-Present**

## Overview

Developed and delivered multi-platform learning solutions for a healthcare technology product used by client organizations and internal teams. The learning experience supported product adoption, onboarding, certification, and ongoing user proficiency in a complex clinical and operational environment.

## Challenge

The client needed learning experiences that would help users understand complex workflows, apply product functionality in real situations, and maintain competency over time. Training also had to support certification programs, continuing education requirements, and varying user roles across multiple groups.

## Approach

I partnered with SMEs, stakeholders, and product owners to translate technical workflows into learner-centered experiences. The work included needs analysis, storyboarding, content development, assessment design, LMS publishing, and reporting support.

### Solution components
- Interactive eLearning modules using Articulate Rise 360 and Storyline 360
- Scenario-based learning grounded in real-world healthcare workflows
- Certification training and learning path design
- Video tutorials, screen recordings, captions, and performance support resources
- LMS administration and reporting for learner progress and completion

## Results

The learning solutions supported product adoption, certification goals, and ongoing learner proficiency by providing consistent, role-appropriate guidance across multiple delivery formats. The work also supported learning operations, reporting, and ongoing content updates as product functionality evolved.

## Why it mattered

This work demonstrated the value of combining instructional design, technology fluency, and operational understanding. The result was a learning experience that helped people not only understand the product, but use it effectively in the real world.
